About Fylde Ship

Who We Are

Fylde Ship Management is a dynamic start-up company in the maritime sector, bringing innovative and cost-effective solutions to offshore and main fleet shipping operations.

We currently own and operate two crew boats, MV Gomti & MV Mahi, each with a capacity of forty-six passengers plus ten crew members. These vessels are chartered with L&T Hydrocarbon Engineering Ltd and operate in the Mumbai High offshore field.
